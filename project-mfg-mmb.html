Munich Mushroom Bridge Art intervention


3D renderings: Julian Mößnang

Management: Susanne Schmalhofer

MMB

Project

Munich mushroom bridge - Art intervention in public space. Concept pitch for the Munich Creative Business Week 2024 with the topic "How to co-create with nature?". The installation is intended to act as a satellite for the event and should attract as much attention as possible. The simple form acts as a symbol of change. Let’s walk the path to a more sustainable future. From close up can you recognise that the bridge is made of a natural material. Mushroom mycelium in the form of bricks in combination with a substructure form the installation. People should cross the bridge and experience the sustainable material with an additional exhibition.
