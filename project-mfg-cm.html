CM

Project

A unified visual identity was developed for “Campus Munich,” bringing together three previously disconnected universities under one cohesive brand. The project included a redesigned spatial system and a clear, accessible wayfinding concept to support diverse academic fields—from design to business to medicine, in collaboration with Sophia Rhein. Based on the dynamic “M” from the logo, a custom typeface and pictogram system were created. A modular grid structure ensures visual consistency while reflecting the campus’s diversity, enabling a bold, flexible, and expressive communication system across all applications—from signage to architectural elements.
