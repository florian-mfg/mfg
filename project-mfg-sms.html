SMW – 2024

Social Music Streaming Website


Designed with Sophia Rhein

SMS is a design concept for a social music platform that connects people through their taste in music. Instead of listening in isolation, users can discover what people around them are listening to, exchange songs, find concerts and connect with others through shared musical interests. The app connects to existing streaming services and transforms the personal music library into a social profile — the individual “SMS Card.” Users can choose between three different visual identities, making the interface itself part of their personal expression. Music becomes a starting point for spontaneous encounters and new friendships: discover listeners nearby, explore their favorite songs and artists, share recommendations or connect through concerts and locations.
